# Near-collinear (flux-tube OPE) expansion of the six-gluon MHV amplitude at order T^2 F^0, planar N=4 SYM.
#
# KINEMATICS (arXiv:1903.10890): u = F/D, v = F S^2/((1+T^2) D), w = T^2/(1+T^2), D = F + F S^2 + S T + F^2 S T + F T^2,
#   T = exp(-tau), S = exp(sigma), F = exp(i phi);  y = S^2;  lny = Log[y] = 2 Log[S];  g^2 = lambda/(16 pi^2);  z_n = zeta(n).
#
# QUANTITY: C = the F^0 twist-two content of the framed hexagon Wilson loop W (BSV III, arXiv:1402.3307 eq. (53)):
#   W = 1 + T (F+1/F) W_[1] + T^2 [ (F^2+1/F^2) W_[2] + C ] + O(T^3),
#   C = 2 W_{F12} + W_{phi phi} + W^{LL}_{psi psibar} + W_{F Fbar},
# with  2 W_{F12}: the effective twist-2 excitation F_{+-} (large fermion at u + small fermion at u-2i),
#       2 W_{F12} = 2 Int_{R+i0} du/(2pi) mu_{F12}(u) T^{E_{F12}(u)-2} S^{i p_{F12}(u)},  mu_{F12} = pi g^2/(u sinh(pi u)) [1 + g^2 m(u) + ...]
#       (BSV III eqs. (49)-(52)); starts at ONE loop and is the ONLY F^0 contribution at LL (k = L-1);
#       W_{phi phi}, W^{LL}_{psi psibar}, W_{F Fbar}: two-particle singlet pairs (scalars, large fermions, gluon-antigluon
#       = W_[1,-1]); all start at TWO loops (g^4, BSV III eq. (54)) and therefore first enter at NLL (k = L-2) with
#       Born integrands x one-loop energies.
# CONVERSIONS (exact in g):
#   Log[W]|_{T^2 F^0} = C - (W_[1])^2            [F^0 part of -(1/2) (F+1/F)^2 W_[1]^2]
#   R6|_{T^2 F^0}     = Log[W]|_{T^2 F^0} - (Gcusp/4) X|_{T^2 F^0},   X|_{T^2 F^0} = -2 (Log[1+y] - lny)
#   E1|_{T^2 F^0}     = X|_{T^2 F^0}  (identical, like at T^1 and T^2 F^{+-2}),  hence
#   E|_{T^2 F^0}      = E|_{T^0} * C   EXACTLY,  with E|_{T^0} = rho^-1 exp[(Gcusp/4)(-2 Log[T]^2 - lny^2/2 - 2 z2)],
#   i.e. through O(T^2) and for every power of F:  E / E|_{T^0} = W  (the -(1/2)(F+1/F)^2 W_[1]^2 of Log[W] is cancelled
#   by the square of the T^1 term when exponentiating).
# VALIDATION: C reproduces BSV III Table 1 (all y^0, y^1 terms through g^8: k = L-1 and L-2 for L <= 4) and the
#   three-loop T^2 F^0 terms of R6 from arXiv:1308.2276 (ancillary R63_T2.m) at Log[T]^2 and Log[T]^1 through y^10
#   (1308.2276 expands in a = 2 g^2: its a^L coefficient = 2^(-L) x ours).  W_[1] needed for R6 is in hexagon_T1_OPE_LL_NLL.txt.
#
# THIS FILE: closed forms of the LEADING-LOG coefficients  CT2F0[L,L-1] = coefficient of g^(2L) T^2 Log[T]^(L-1) F^0 in W
#   (= 2 W_{F12}^(L) at Log[T]^(L-1)), written as CT2F0[L,k] = CT2F0A[L,k]/y + CT2F0B[L,k] + y*CT2F0C[L,k], A,B,C pure
#   G-functions Iy(a1,...,an) = G(a1,...,an; y) with indices in {0,-1} (G(0,..,0;y) = Log[y]^n/n!) times zeta values.
#   Fitted mod a 61-bit prime from the series through y^3300 and re-expanded exactly against exact series through y^40.
